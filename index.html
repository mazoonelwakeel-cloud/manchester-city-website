About Manchester City

Manchester City Football Club is a professional football club based in Manchester, England. Founded in 1880, the club has become one of the most successful teams in world football. Manchester City is known for its attacking style of play, world-class players, and historic achievements under manager Pep Guardiola. The club won its first UEFA Champions League title in 2023 and completed a historic continental treble by winning the Premier League, FA Cup, and Champions League in the same season.

Journey to Glory

  • Early Years (1880–2008): Manchester City was founded in 1880 as St. Mark's before becoming Manchester City in 1894. The club experienced success during the late 1960s and early 1970s, winning league titles, FA Cups, the League Cup, and the European Cup Winners' Cup.
  • The Takeover Era (2008–2016): A new chapter began in 2008 when the club was purchased by Abu Dhabi United Group. Major investments brought players such as Sergio Agüero, David Silva, Vincent Kompany, and Yaya Touré, leading to the dramatic Premier League title win in 2012.
  • The Guardiola Revolution (2016–2022): Pep Guardiola transformed Manchester City into one of the greatest football teams in the world. City became the first Premier League club to reach 100 points in a single season, earning the nickname "Centurions".
  • The Treble-Winning Era (2022–2023): Led by Erling Haaland, Kevin De Bruyne, Rodri, and Pep Guardiola, Manchester City won the Premier League, FA Cup, and UEFA Champions League, completing a historic continental treble.
  • Modern Dominance (2023–Present): Manchester City continues to dominate English football. In 2024 the club became the first team in English football history to win four consecutive Premier League titles.
